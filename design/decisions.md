# Design Decisions

- 2026-09-07 · Eyes must be embedded into the head silhouette rather than floating above it · The owner rejected the protruding-eye treatment after seeing it in motion.
- 2026-09-07 · Desktop should offer mouse or keyboard steering, with equivalent touch play on mobile and hold-to-boost · Control parity is more important than customization polish.
- 2026-09-07 · Keep the Decentraland avatar outside the playable arena view · The snake is the player's game identity and the avatar currently obscures the field.
- 2026-09-07 · Reduce the snake's world scale so the arena can support many simultaneous players · Crowd readability matters more than a large individual creature.
- 2026-09-07 · Use compact, overlapping round segments with a soft aqua arena and expressive head details as the visual reference · Preserve the supplied reference's clarity while creating original assets.
- 2026-09-07 · The snake should read as one continuous body rather than separated spheres · The current segmented gaps weaken the creature fantasy.
- 2026-09-07 · Replace the fixed overhead view with a moving 3D camera · The owner wants depth and motion while gameplay remains horizontal.
- 2026-09-07 · Use a Decentraland-canon visual language for the colorful arena and snakes · Make the experience feel native to Decentraland rather than like an imported clone.
- 2026-09-07 · Snakes move only on a horizontal plane inside a 3D environment · Preserve the readable movement and spatial strategy of classic snake games.
- 2026-09-07 · Use a colorful visual style and customizable snakes · Player expression should be immediately visible in the arena.
